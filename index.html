Bio

I work on applied econometrics questions in Indian political economy and development — currently on labour-market outcomes of India's energy transition, fiscal constraints behind out-migration in Bihar, and measurement issues in informality across the EUS–PLFS transition. I hold an M.Sc. in Economics from Central University of Andhra Pradesh and am UGC-NET qualified. My work is grounded in panel and time-series methods — staggered DiD, Synthetic Control, GMM, and DCC-GARCH — applied to Indian state-panel and household survey data.

Results

Headline findings from completed and near-complete work.

Renewable-investment states: +4.2 to +6.8 pp formal employment over five years. Coal phase-down states: −7.3 to −9.1 pp real wages, limited mean reversion. Effects concentrated within 150 km of project sites.

Negative interaction between education and mining dependence — consistent with a talent-misallocation channel, where higher education amplifies the resource curse by redirecting skilled individuals toward rent-seeking. Robust to Difference GMM and alternative instruments.
